Privacy Policy - Morph Launcher

Last updated: August 31, 2026
Public policy for Morph Launcher on Google Play. Processing follows the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
Also: Vectr privacy

1. Operator and applicable law

Morph Launcher is developed and published by DMTech Softworks (the Operator). This Policy covers the mobile app Morph Launcher and related public materials on this website.

Personal data is processed in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan, including the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated 21 May 2013 No. 94-V “On Personal Data and Their Protection”, and other applicable acts of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Where Google Play or other platforms impose additional disclosure requirements, this page also serves as the public privacy notice for those stores.

2. What Morph Launcher is

Morph Launcher is an Android home-screen launcher: app drawer, dock, clock/widgets area, icon packs, wallpaper, optional PIN lock for apps, and related local preferences. The app runs on your device. DMTech Softworks does not operate a cloud account system for Morph Launcher and does not sync your launcher layout or PIN to its own servers.

3. What data may be processed

  • On device: launcher settings (language, dock/drawer appearance, theme options); app order; list of hidden packages; optional app-lock PIN stored as a salted hash (not the raw PIN in cleartext); optional custom wallpaper file in app storage; selected icon-pack preference.
  • Installed apps (on device only): Morph Launcher reads the list of launchable apps from the system package manager to show icons and labels on the home screen and in the drawer. This stays on your device and is not sent to DMTech Softworks.
  • Contact channels: if you write to Telegram or email, we process the message content and contact details you send, solely to respond.
  • We do not require registration, do not sell personal data, and do not build advertising profiles from Morph Launcher usage.
  • Morph Launcher does not declare the Internet permission and does not phone home for analytics, ads, or crash-reporting cloud services operated by DMTech Softworks.

4. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Provide the launcher’s functions on your device (home screen, drawer, dock, wallpaper, icon packs, hide apps, optional PIN lock, double-tap lock via device-admin when you enable it).
  • Respond to support and other messages you send.
  • Comply with legal obligations of the Republic of Kazakhstan where they apply.

Processing is based on: your actions/consent when you change settings, hide apps, set a PIN, pick wallpaper or icon packs, grant device-admin for lock, or contact us; performance of the service you request; and legitimate interests of the Operator that do not override your rights (for example securing the app and answering inquiries), within the limits of RK law.

5. Cross-border transfer

DMTech Softworks does not transfer your launcher preferences, hidden-app list, PIN hash, or wallpaper to its own servers — in Kazakhstan or abroad. The app is designed to keep that data on the device. If you contact us by Telegram or email, the message may be processed via those providers under their own terms.

6. Retention

  • Local app data remains on the device until you clear app data, reset settings, or uninstall Morph Launcher.
  • Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request, then deleted or archived in a limited form if required by law.

7. Your rights (Republic of Kazakhstan)

Under RK personal data law you may, as applicable, request information about processing, access, correction, blocking, or destruction of personal data relating to you that we hold (for example in support correspondence), and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent — unless another ground under RK law still applies. To exercise rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within reasonable time under applicable RK rules.

8. App lock (optional PIN)

  • Off until you set a passkey in Settings.
  • The PIN is stored as a salted cryptographic hash on device; Morph Launcher does not upload it.
  • You are responsible for remembering the PIN. Clearing app data removes the lock data.

9. Device admin / double-tap lock (optional)

  • To turn the screen off with a double-tap gesture, Morph Launcher may ask you to enable a device-admin receiver that can force-lock the screen.
  • This permission is used only for the lock gesture you enable. You can revoke device-admin in system settings at any time.

10. Permissions summary

  • Vibrate — optional haptics / feedback.
  • Set wallpaper — when you choose a custom wallpaper.
  • Request delete packages — when you choose to uninstall a user app from the launcher menu (system confirmation still applies).
  • Request ignore battery optimizations — optional, so the launcher can stay responsive as the home app.
  • Device admin (force-lock) — only if you enable double-tap screen off and grant it in the system dialog.
  • Query launchable apps — to list home-screen apps without broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

11. Children

Morph Launcher is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data via support channels, contact us and we will delete it where required.

12. Security

We apply reasonable organizational and technical measures appropriate to a local-first launcher (including hashed PIN storage on device where implemented). You remain responsible for protecting your device and PIN. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Morph Launcher — Android-лаунчер домашнего экрана: drawer, dock, часы/виджеты, icon packs, обои, опциональный PIN-лок приложений и локальные настройки. Приложение работает на твоём устройстве.   • Установленные приложения (только на устройстве): Morph Launcher читает список запускаемых приложений через системный package manager, чтобы показать иконки и названия. Это остаётся на устройстве и не уходит на серверы DMTech Softworks.

  • Регистрация не требуется, персональные данные не продаём, рекламные профили по использованию Morph Launcher не строим.
  • Morph Launcher не заявляет разрешение Internet и не «звонит домой» за аналитикой, рекламой или облачным crash-reporting со стороны DMTech Softworks.

8. App lock (опциональный PIN)

  • Выключен, пока ты сам не задашь passkey в настройках.
  • PIN хранится на устройстве как salted cryptographic hash; Morph Launcher его не загружает в сеть.
  • Запомнить PIN — на тебе. Очистка данных приложения снимает lock.

9. Device admin / double-tap lock (опционально)

  • Чтобы гасить экран двойным тапом, Morph Launcher может попросить включить device-admin с правом force-lock.
  • Разрешение используется только для жеста блокировки, который ты включил. Device-admin можно отозвать в системных настройках в любой момент.

10. Разрешения

  • Vibrate — опциональный haptic / отклик.
  • Set wallpaper — когда выбираешь свои обои.
  • Request delete packages — когда выбираешь удаление пользовательского приложения из меню лаунчера (системное подтверждение всё равно нужно).
  • Request ignore battery optimizations — опционально, чтобы лаунчер оставался отзывчивым как home-приложение.
  • Device admin (force-lock) — только если включишь double-tap screen off и подтвердишь в системном диалоге.
  • Query launchable apps — список приложений для home без широкого QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.
